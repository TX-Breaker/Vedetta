## English

**App name:** Vedetta
**Subtitle (30 chars):** Cameras & NVR for your Mac
**Tagline:** Your security cameras, native on the Mac.

**Short description (80 chars):**
Live view, recordings and MP4 export for VIGI, Hikvision, Dahua and ONVIF cameras.

**Full description:**
Vedetta is a fast, native Mac app for your security cameras and network video recorders. Watch every
camera live, find any moment on a calendar and a zoomable timeline, and save recordings to your Mac as MP4
without losing quality.

It works with TP-Link VIGI recorders, ONVIF recorders and cameras of any brand (Hikvision, Dahua, Uniview,
Reolink and more) and any RTSP stream. Video is decoded by the Mac's hardware, so sixteen cameras run at a
fraction of the CPU used by Windows software under emulation.

• Live view with 1 to 16 tiles, pages, automatic tour, full screen and multiple monitors
• Recordings with calendar, 24-hour timeline, colour-coded events and synchronized playback of 4 cameras
• MP4 export of any range or event, with no re-encoding
• Motion, person and vehicle events with notifications
• PTZ control with saved positions
• Automatic discovery of devices on your network
• Passwords in the macOS Keychain — no accounts, no cloud, no tracking
• English, Italian and French; follows your Mac's language

**Keywords:** NVR, CCTV, security camera, IP camera, ONVIF, RTSP, VIGI, TP-Link, Hikvision, Dahua, Reolink,
Uniview, surveillance, video recorder, PTZ, live view, playback
**Category:** Video / Utilities · **Price:** Free, open source (MIT)

## Screenshots checklist (add to `docs/images/` before announcing)

1. `live-grid.png` — Live view, 9 or 16 cameras, sidebar visible.
2. `playback.png` — Recordings: calendar, 2×2 synchronized playback, timeline with events.
3. `events.png` — Events of the day with filters.
4. `welcome.png` — First launch ("What do you want to connect?").
5. `ptz.png` — PTZ panel on a motorized camera.

Take them with ⇧⌘5 → window capture (hold ⌥ to remove the shadow), at least 2560×1600, in light and dark mode.
Blur or crop anything that shows your home, faces or licence plates.
